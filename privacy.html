Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Short Video Maker is a personal tool used by its owner to create short videos and upload them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. This policy explains how the App handles data.

Information we access

When the owner connects TikTok through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token and the basic profile information of the connected account (display name and avatar) through the user.info.basic scope. The App uses the video.upload scope only to send video files created by the owner to the owner's TikTok drafts.

How information is used

The access token is used only to perform the actions requested by the owner inside the App. Profile information is only displayed to show which account is connected.

Storage and sharing

The access token and any videos are stored locally on the owner's own device or server. We do not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties. We do not collect data about any other TikTok users.

Data retention and deletion

The owner can delete the stored token at any time by deleting the App's local token file, and can revoke the App's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.
